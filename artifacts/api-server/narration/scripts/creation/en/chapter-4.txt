### lessons
[warmly] Story Lessons.
### lesson0
[reverent] Lesson 1. God Is the Creator of Animal Life. The account attributes the creation of sea creatures, birds, and land animals to God. Living creatures are presented as part of His creative work.
### lesson1
[cheerfully] Lesson 2. Creation Contains Great Variety. The chapter describes creatures in the waters, birds in the sky, and animals on the land. [warmly] This variety reveals the richness of the world presented in the creation account.
### lesson2
[warmly] Lesson 3. God Declares His Creation Good. After creating the sea creatures, birds, and land animals, God sees that His work is good. The account consistently presents creation as something He values.
### lesson3
[tenderly] Lesson 4. God’s Blessing Includes Fruitfulness. God blesses the sea creatures and birds and commands them to multiply. The continuation and abundance of life are part of the purpose described in the passage.
### lesson4
[calm] Lesson 5. Living Creatures Have a Place Within God’s Creation. The animals occupy the environments God established. The waters, sky, and land are filled with different kinds of living creatures, each belonging to the larger order of creation.
