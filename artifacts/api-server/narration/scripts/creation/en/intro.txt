### label
[warmly] Introduction.
### title
[warmly, storytelling] Creation: The Beginning of Everything. [reverent] God is the Creator of all things. His creation reveals His power, wisdom, order, goodness, and purpose.
### p0
[hushed, slow storytelling voice] Before the first sunrise, before the oceans stretched across the earth, before the stars illuminated the night sky, and before humanity took its first breath... [whispers] there was God.
### p1
[softly] There were no cities, no mountains covered in forests, no birds singing in the trees, and no waves breaking upon the shore. [calm] The world as we know it did not yet exist.
### p2
[dramatic pause] [awed] Then, at the beginning of everything, God acted.
### p3
[full of wonder] The opening of the Bible takes us to the foundation of existence itself. In six days, God brings order out of what is described as formless and empty. [building excitement] He commands light to shine, separates the waters, gathers the seas, brings forth vegetation, places lights in the heavens, fills the waters and skies with living creatures, and creates animals upon the land.
### p4
[hushed, reverent] Then comes the most extraordinary moment of creation: [awed] God makes humanity in His own image, giving human beings a unique place and responsibility within His creation.
### p5
[warmly] At the end of His work, God looks upon everything He has made [joyful] and declares it VERY good. [softly] On the seventh day, He rests from His work of creation and blesses the day.
### p6
[calm, with conviction] This is the story of the beginning — not merely of the world, but of the foundations upon which the biblical story unfolds. [reverent] It introduces us to the God who creates, brings order, gives life, establishes purpose, and declares His creation good.
### p7
[warmly, inviting] Through these six chapters, we will journey through the creation account, exploring what happened at each stage [cheerfully] and discovering the lessons, reflections, and truths that the beginning of everything offers us today.
